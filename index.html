About

I'm a web developer from Pakistan, building websites since 2020 and Shopify stores and web apps for brands in the UK, the USA and beyond.

Most of my work is on Shopify: building stores, designing themes with custom sections, and fixing the things that slow a store down or stop people buying. When a project needs more than a store, I build dashboards and web apps in Next.js.

I studied computer science at Minhaj University Lahore (BSc, 2020 to 2022). I keep communication simple: clear scope, regular updates and work delivered on time.
